# Hackathon Brief

Source: `https://redditgameswithahook.devpost.com/` and `/rules`, read on June 21, 2026.

## Event

Reddit's Games with a Hook Hackathon asks builders to create a new game on the Reddit Developer Platform. The event is virtual from June 17, 2026 to July 15, 2026. Submissions close July 15, 2026 at 6:00 PM PDT.

The project must use Devvit / Reddit Developer Platform. The game should run inside Reddit, usually as a Devvit Web app in a Reddit post.

## Prize signals

Prize categories reveal what judges want:

- Best App with a Hook: a strong, original, sticky core idea.
- Best Use of Phaser: meaningful use of Phaser, not just adding the dependency.
- Best Use of Retention Mechanics: a reason to return tomorrow.
- Best Use of User Contributions: players or communities add value to future play.
- Honorable mentions reward polished, complete, interesting projects.

## Judging signals

Judging criteria include:

- Delightful UX.
- Polish.
- Reddit-y design.
- Hook-y design.
- Phaser innovation.

## Required submission pieces

- Devpost project submission.
- Reddit app listing.
- Demo post link in a public subreddit, usually under 200 members unless app review/install rules allow otherwise.
- Root `README.md`.
- Project must be free and available during judging.

Optional:

- Demo video under 1 minute.
- Public source repository.
- Devvit feedback survey.

## What they do not want

Avoid:

- Obvious AI-generated slop.
- Literal or shallow "hook" interpretations.
- Generic Reddit-themed reskins.
- Common clones such as basic space shooters or platformers unless the hook is genuinely novel.
- Generic collaborative storytelling/trivia unless the interaction is exceptional.

## What they do want

Prefer:

- Bite-sized play in a Reddit feed.
- Daily or weekly reasons to return.
- Meaningful choices.
- Progression, streaks, unlocks, rankings, or community milestones.
- Async community play.
- Safe player contributions that change future sessions.
- Clear first-time play with strong visual feedback.
